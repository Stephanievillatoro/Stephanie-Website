About Me

A brief overview of my background, academic honors, and leadership focus.

I am an ambitious Ogden Honors College and E.J. Ourso College of Business student at Louisiana State University, pursuing a double major in Accounting and Information Systems & Analytics with a concentration in Analytics, holding a 3.875 GPA. My goal is to use the skills and knowledge I gain from both degrees to bridge accounting with information systems and make a meaningful impact in diverse communities.

I am especially passionate about helping underrepresented Latino communities gain financial understanding and knowledge to properly run personal businesses. Growing up bilingual in English and Spanish, I understand firsthand the barriers many families face in navigating financial systems, and I want to be a resource that helps close that gap. Beyond the classroom, I serve as a CAS Tutor at LSU, Treasurer for the Latin American Student Organization (LASO), and operate independent e-commerce resale businesses across Depop and Vinted.
